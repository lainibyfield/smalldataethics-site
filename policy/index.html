How Small Data Ethics extends policy

  • Policy often emphasizes legality and privacy. Small Data Ethics additionally emphasizes fairness, incentives, and repair.
  • Compliance can be achieved while harm still occurs. The operational goal is not "compliant" — it is "defensible, explainable, and correctable."
